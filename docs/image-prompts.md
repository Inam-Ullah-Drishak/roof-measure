# Image Generation Brief: Roof Measure Website

You are creating photographs for the website of **Roof Measure**, a US company that sells aerial roof measurement reports to roofing contractors, insurance adjusters and solar installers.

Please create **all 14 images listed below**, one image per item. Generate them one at a time, in order, and label each result with its **file name** so it can be saved correctly.

---

## Rules for every image

1. **No text of any kind.** No words, letters, numbers, signs, labels, logos, watermarks or captions anywhere in the image.
2. **No recognizable faces.** People may appear only from behind, from a distance, or as hands, unless an item says otherwise.
3. **Photorealistic.** Every image must look like a real professional photograph, not an illustration, 3D render or cartoon.
4. **Green screens stay plain.** When an item mentions a green screen, the device screen must be a flat, even, bright green (#00FF00) with no reflections, glare, icons or content.
5. **Use the size and shape given** for each image (landscape unless stated).
6. **Keep the same style for all 14 images** (see below), so they look like one photo shoot.

## Shared style (apply to every image)

Professional commercial photography, natural daylight, clean and modern, slightly cool color grade with deep navy blue (#0a1433) tones in the shadows and warm amber (#f59e0b) tones in the highlights, sharp focus, high detail, realistic materials, premium and trustworthy feel, American suburban and commercial setting.
